## Revenue Model

### Subscription Tiers

#### Free Tier (Freemium)
- **Price**: $0/month
- **Target**: Individual users, trials
- **Features**: Basic analytics, 10 AI insights/month
- **Conversion Goal**: 10% to paid tiers

#### Starter ($49/month)
- **Target**: Small businesses
- **Features**: Advanced analytics, 100 insights/month
- **Annual Discount**: 20% ($470/year)
- **Target Customers**: 500 in Year 1

#### Professional ($199/month)
- **Target**: Growing companies
- **Features**: Unlimited analytics and insights
- **Annual Discount**: 20% ($1,910/year)
- **Target Customers**: 300 in Year 1

#### Enterprise (Custom)
- **Starting Price**: $1,000/month
- **Target**: Large organizations
- **Features**: Everything + custom development
- **Target Customers**: 20 in Year 1
